# Courts After Dark — Athlete & Operator User Guide

A complete reference guide to fair-use policies, reservation rules, and facility operations.

---

## 1. Fair-Use & Anti-Monopoly Policy

To ensure equitable nocturnal play across high-demand sports facilities, Courts After Dark enforces algorithmic anti-monopoly caps:

### 1.1 Active Reservation Quota (Max 2 Active Bookings)
- Each athlete is limited to a maximum of **2 active (Confirmed) bookings** at any given moment.
- Once a reserved match is completed or cancelled, the slot quota automatically unlocks.
- This prevents automated bots or single teams from reserving entire weeks of court time in advance.

### 1.2 Prime-Time Fair Allocation (18:00 – 21:00 Peak Cap)
- Hours between **6:00 PM and 9:00 PM** are classified as **Prime-Time (Peak)** and marked with an amber flame icon (`🔥`).
- Athletes may hold at most **3 prime-time reservations per calendar week**.
- Off-peak slots (06:00 – 18:00 and 21:00 – 23:00) do not count against the weekly prime-time cap.

---

## 2. Cancellation & Refund Policy

- **Free Cancellation Window:** You can cancel a reservation up to **4 hours** before the scheduled start time directly from your **My Bookings** dashboard.
- **Immediate Quota Restoration:** Upon cancellation, your active quota is instantly restored.
- **Automated Waitlist Notification:** If other athletes are queued on the waitlist for that time slot, the first athlete in the FIFO queue is automatically alerted.

---

## 3. Sports Equipment Rentals

- **Attached to Court:** Gear (e.g. tournament badminton rackets, match footballs, volleyballs, cricket bats) can be checked out during court booking.
- **Standalone Rentals:** Gear can also be rented independently from the `/equipment` catalog.
- **Return Verification:** Operators verify gear condition upon return, restoring inventory counters automatically.

---

## 4. Multi-Gateway Payments

Courts After Dark provides two payment options:
1. **Credit / Debit Cards:** Instant payment with cardholder name, 16-digit card number, MM/YY expiry, and CVV.
2. **Razorpay Gateway:** Native support for Indian UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), NetBanking, and mobile wallets.
